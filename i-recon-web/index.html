Detection pipeline

Face landmarks drive intentional communication gestures. A separate pose model watches for rapid downward movement followed by a collapsed posture. A confirmed fall becomes a Communication event and is also sent to the configured caregiver alert channel.

Engineering prototype — not a medical device. Fall thresholds should be validated for the intended environment and user.
